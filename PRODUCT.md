# Product

## Register

product

## Users

The general public, on both phone and desktop, using the Free and Pro plans. They log everyday expenses and keep track of money they have lent to other people, including installment schedules with partial payments. Most entries happen in the moment, on a phone, between other things; the desktop is where they review.

## Product Purpose

Chiro is a personal expense and loan tracker. It records income and expenses with categories and tags, sets budgets per category, and manages loans with payment calendars, partial payments and cascading restructures. Success means a user can log an expense or a payment in seconds and read what they owe, what is owed to them, and what is left at a glance.

## Brand Personality

Agile, precise, sober. The voice is direct and quiet: it states the number and the next action, with no cheerleading and no urgency theatre. Emotional goal: control and trust when handling real money.

## Anti-references

- The default shadcn template: all zinc, black buttons, no identity.
- Cold corporate banking: navy and gold, formal and distant.
- Neon fintech: gradients, glows and saturated color on dark backgrounds.
- The overloaded dashboard: many cards and charts competing before the content.

Positive references: the clear, large numbers and strong hierarchy of Revolut and Wise; the speed and low friction of Linear and Raycast; the calm, system-native feel of Apple Notes and Wallet.

## Design Principles

1. **Capture in seconds.** The primary action is always one tap away, and common entries need the fewest fields possible.
2. **Numbers first.** Hierarchy comes from the amount, not from decoration. Totals, balances and installments are readable at a glance.
3. **Quiet by default.** One accent color; other color is reserved for state (paid, pending, overdue, error).
4. **One vocabulary.** The same component does the same job on every screen: one button shape, one form-control style, one icon set.
5. **Mobile first.** Base styles target the phone; the desktop is an expansion of the same design, not a second one.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA in both light and dark themes. Touch targets are at least 44px. Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`. State is never conveyed by color alone: paid and pending items also carry text or an icon. The interface is available in Spanish and English.
